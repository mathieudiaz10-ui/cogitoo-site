Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

🔒 No data collection app

Our Commitment

Cogitoo is an educational app for children aged 3 to 6. Protecting children's privacy is our top priority.

In short: Cogitoo does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Data Collected

No personal data is collected.

More specifically, Cogitoo:

  • Does not require any registration or account creation
  • Does not collect names, emails, or other identifying information
  • Does not collect location data
  • Does not collect photos or content created by the child
  • Does not use cookies or trackers
  • Does not integrate any third-party analytics tools

Locally Stored Data

The following information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted:

  • Progress in games
  • Avatar customization
  • Full version purchase status

This data can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app.

In-App Purchases

The app offers a one-time purchase to unlock the full version ($1.99). This purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store.

We do not receive or store any payment information. Only the "purchase made or not" status is kept locally on your device.

Parental protection: Parental verification (math calculation) is required before any purchase or access to external links.

Advertising

Cogitoo contains no advertising, whether targeted or not.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties, because we do not collect any data.

The app does not contain any third-party SDKs for tracking, analytics, or advertising.

External Links

The app may contain links to:

  • This privacy policy
  • Our terms of use
  • Our support page

All these links are protected by parental verification and open in the device's external browser.

Compliance

Cogitoo is designed in compliance with:

  • App Store guidelines for apps in the Kids category
  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new update date.

We encourage you to review this page regularly to stay informed.
